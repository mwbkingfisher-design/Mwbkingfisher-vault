As soon as they had left Pier called the police and the emergency service.

“Sir? You can't be here. Please leave!” The guard were coming their way. The noise was enough to wake him up from his sleep. 

“I can be here, but the ladies here shouldn’t. Lady Jill had kept them against their will.”

“That can't be. Impossible.” he said, but at the same time he drew out a phone and dialed someone.

“Miss Jill? Yes sorry…” The guard was nervous. Lady Jill must have been exasperated. Not often her captives were broken free. The phantom of the consequences she would have to deal with probably made her anxious.

It wasn’t long time when time heard loud police sirens. The guard was making a lot of gestures that mixed with his twisted expression.

They sat on the stairs in front of the magnificent villa. Girls looked as if they never had been kidnapped. Even though they were kept against their will, Lady Jill cared about them.

“Alright lets wait for the police on the street.” said Pier.

They stood up, but when they were about to leave, the guard stood in their way.

“Wait,” he said. “Lady Jill want to talk to you girls.”

Julianne jumped, as huge grin brightened her face. “Yes! Let me talk to mum!”

Kate was reluctant to talk, but Pier saw that she didn’t want to leave Julianne alone in this. The guard turned on the speaker and passed the phone to the girls.

“Juls? Katie? Are you there?” Shaky Jill’s voice came from the speaker.

“Yes mum! Are you coming to us? We’d left from our castle! Mr. Pier took us out!” Julianne jumped a bit. 

That was sweet, but Pier couldn’t get of his mind surrealistic situation he had before his eyes. A teenager girl behaving like literal child.

“No sweetie. I’d love to, but I don’t think I’ll join. Anytime soon.”

They heard quiet sobs coming from the other side.

“Mum, are you okay?”

“Yes, don’t worry, I thought about something sad.” She tried to laugh and it didn’t sound right. But for Julianne it was enough.
